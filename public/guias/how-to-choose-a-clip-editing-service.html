How to choose a clip editing service (and what to ask before you hire)

Before hiring a clip editor or agency, check who actually edits each clip, the turnaround, what the price includes (quantity, captions, formats), how your files are handled and whether payment is one-time or a subscription.

What to check

  • Is it edited by a person or automatic? Ask for real examples of finished clips.
  • A clear turnaround time, in writing.
  • How many clips the price includes and from how many minutes of video.
  • Captions, your name on screen and vertical or horizontal format: are they included?
  • One-time payment or subscription: pick what fits your posting rhythm.
  • What happens to your videos: where they are stored and when they are deleted.
  • How to complain if something goes wrong.

Red flags

  • They promise virality or a view count: nobody can guarantee that.
  • No examples and no explanation of who edits.
  • They ask for your account passwords (not needed; a public link is enough).

FAQ

What does a clip package usually include?

A number of vertical clips with captions and a delivery time; the rest (name on screen, horizontal format, revisions) depends on the service.

Is it better to pay per clip or per package?

A package usually gives a lower price per clip and a set turnaround; paying per clip is good for trying with a small order.

Do they guarantee the clip will go viral?

No serious service can. What can be promised is edit quality, turnaround and good treatment.
