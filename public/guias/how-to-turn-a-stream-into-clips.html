How to turn a stream or podcast into clips for TikTok, Shorts and Reels

Pick the moments with the strongest reaction, cut them to vertical 9:16 at 20 to 60 seconds, add large captions and a hook in the first 2 seconds, then post to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Reels. If you do not have time, an agency like streamlogics does it for you.

1. Find the moments

  • Look for laughs, surprises, arguments, plays or lines that make sense without context.
  • Check where chat or audio spikes: something usually happened there.
  • A good clip tells one idea and lands in under a minute.

2. Make it vertical

  • Use 9:16 (1080×1920). Keep the person or the action in the center.
  • Recommended length: 20 to 60 seconds.

3. Captions and hook

  • Large, readable captions: many people watch without sound.
  • Put the best part, or a question, in the first 2 seconds.
  • Keep text away from the face and the action.

4. Post and repeat

  • Upload the same clip to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, adjusting title and hashtags.
  • Consistency matters: several clips a week beat one stray clip.

FAQ

How long should a TikTok or Shorts clip be?

Between 20 and 60 seconds works well for most cases; what matters is that the clip tells a single idea.

Can I make clips from a podcast?

Yes. The strongest segments are picked, turned vertical with captions and posted just like stream clips.

Do I need to send the full video?

At streamlogics a public link (Twitch, Kick, YouTube, Drive or Dropbox) and the part you want worked on is enough.
